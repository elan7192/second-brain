# Playbook: outreach that does not sound like outreach

From [[src-deronin-growthos-vault]]. Hub: [[growth-playbooks]]. **Do not send.** File drafts only.

You do not write "hi, I offer services." You write to the person about a hole they already see.

## Shape

1. Name one specific piece of their work (video, hook, craft).
2. Name the hole: no offer, no checkout, views that do not sell.
3. One line on the system: distribution plus a Whop offer, rev-share, they keep the skill.
4. Ask for a 15-minute look at their last 10 videos. No deck.

## What already failed

Generic "I can grow your account" got ignored on [[growth-partner-mira]] first pass. Specific hook critique got a reply.
