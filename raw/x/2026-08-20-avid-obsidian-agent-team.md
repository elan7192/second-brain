## Tweet

Karpathy-style LLM wiki for every bot, every decision, and every piece of research.

Setup in 13 steps:

1. One Obsidian vault. `Home.md` is the door.
2. Hunt folders: twitter, github, hacker-news, reddit, product-hunt, inbox.
3. Ship folders: drafts, digests, angles, builds.
4. CoS wiki: Index, How-it-works, Today, Decisions.
5. Maps: Jarvis, Hooks, TELOS.
6. Graph colors: gold wiki, teal maps, blue twitter, green github.
7. File first. Every note gets a date and a folder.
8. Scouts write into Hunt and never ping you. Chat only gets a short digest.
9. Nothing in the vault posts, pays, or sends. Final yes stays human.
10. Daily loop: 08:00 IST daily note, agency reports 08:14 / 14:14 / 20:14.
11. Performance writes to twitter/live-score. Staff engineer writes to builds/.
12. Lock decisions in Wiki/Decisions so dead posts are not remade.
13. Earn hooks in Maps/Hooks: trend -> foil -> hook family -> artifact -> lock.

## Quoted article claims (context graph)

Do not fill a million-token window. Give ~20k of the right tokens: entities, relationships, events, decisions.

Similarity is not relationship. Graph retrieval can follow causal chains vector search misses.

Temporal facts need validity windows (Graphiti-style). Stale "works_on" edges poison agents.

Skills are how we do things. The graph is what is currently true.
