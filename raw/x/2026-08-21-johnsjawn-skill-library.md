## Tweet

Every company needs killer Skills library. Not a folder in GitHub that 10% of the company knows how to use. A living library the whole company benefits from.

The best deal review skill gets better every time a sales rep uses it. A great research skill gets discovered instead of rebuilt 50 times. Creating a skill should be as easy as asking to create something useful from based on the context of your work. And the top skills (and skill creators) should rise to the top.

Create, share, collaborate on, discover and understand usage of skills, organize them into databases by team or use case, then use them across any of your agents.

Still early, but the idea is to make Notion the best home for your company’s collective AI skills.

Made a quick demo (all available now)!

## Demo (attached video)

Notion Library, Skills tab. Skills are pages. Visible chrome: Agents, Skills, Recents, Favorites, AI Meeting Notes, Shared, Private. Subfilters: Enabled for me, Discover. Button: New skill.

Table columns: Name, Description, Database, Created by, # Uses, Enable for me.

Databases on screen: Skills, EPD Skills, Marketing Skills, Docs.

Skill pages show a banner: "This page is being used as an AI skill." Example pages: Ivan Bot Skill (pre-review marketing drafts through Ivan's lens), Skill: Voice & Tone Customer Reply Builder (Zendesk email). Comments on pages. Overflow menu: Use automatically ("If enabled, Notion Agent will use this skill automatically when helpful."), Download for local agents, Disable for me.

Spoken in the demo: skills are Notion pages; an agent built Ivan Bot Skill from Ivan's feedback across Notion pages and Slack (generation not shown); organize into different databases; download to local agents and use across any agents you are using; usage column shows which skills get the most use and who is creating the best skills.

Agents tab shown as a gallery of named agents.

Not shown: a skill's instructions changing after a use; a downloaded skill running in a local agent; a creator leaderboard.
