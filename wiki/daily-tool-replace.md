# Daily tool replace

If you know the tool well, replace daily-flow garbage by building your own with an LLM, in an hour tops, without weeks of maintenance or a side-project tax.

Source: [[src-can1357-daily-tool-replace-2026-08-27]]. Author: [[can-boluk]].

## Locked readings

- Hour tops is tweet wording. No timed study. Do not treat it as a vault SLA.
- Keep list in that post: Apple software, ghostty, occasionally Zed, compilers (rustc, LLVM). Everything else in his day-to-day is claimed replaced. Unverified beyond the tweet.
- GitKraken is the named pre-LLM example he would have stayed stuck with.
