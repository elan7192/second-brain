# NMC trace complexity

Aimone: neuromorphic computing does not change the time or space class of an algorithm. Energy can, when activity is sparse.

Source: [[src-arxiv-2507-17886]]. Project: [[arxiv-tierlist]].

## Kept

Algorithm as graph \(G_N=(N,S)\). Time in the ideal parallel limit is critical-path depth. Energy tracks the execution trace, not the scheduled dense work. Sparse decaying spike traces are the energy-win regime. Dense always-on ANN layers are not.

No chip bake-off in the digest. Methods M3–M4 proofs unread.

Do not confuse this "trace" with [[trace]] (Amazon context attribution).
