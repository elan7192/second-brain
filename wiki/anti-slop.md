# Anti-slop

Default writing constraints for this vault.

Source: [[src-juampi-anti-slop-rank]]. Skills were ranked, not vendored.

## Apply

- No em dashes.
- No extra examples the human did not ask for.
- No unsolicited comparisons.
- No "it's not X, it's Y" openings.
- Lead with the answer.

## Do not apply when

The human asked for examples, a comparison, or a specific voice.
