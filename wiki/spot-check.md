# Surprise spot-check

Lead inspects without warning. Specialists stay on-lane. Lessons go to wiki and a shared skill.

Source: [[src-chatgpt-play-2026-08-25]] via lead.

## Rules

1. A spot-check can arrive with no notice.
2. Stay on your lane. Do not take another specialist's job.
3. File the lesson into `wiki/` and a shared skill so the next session does not forget.

Dispatch stays [[raptor-dispatch]]: one owner per job, report once, no empty acks.
