# Grok Bot quota

Grok Bot quota burns fastest on:

1. Cursor cloud agents in Max Mode
2. computerUse screenshot/vision loops
3. Long specialist transcripts reread every turn

Recurring fill belongs on a dedicated bot (`burn`), not the lead chat.

Source: lead method compiled with [[src-benln-grok-bot-pro-tips]] (tips 4, 5, 9). lan E share, not scout.
