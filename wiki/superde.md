# SuperDE

DDQN picks DE mutation and constraint handling each generation. Trained on CEC2010. Still a 28-action Q-net.

Source: [[src-arxiv-2509-11016]]. Project: [[arxiv-tierlist]].

## Kept

22-d handcrafted state. 4 mutations × 7 CHTs. Zero-shot to CEC2010-ext, CEC2017, G2000, BBOB2022. Often better Wilcoxon than listed DE variants. Several problems stay infeasible for all methods.

Not a general foundation model. No discrete/combinatorial COPs in the digest.
