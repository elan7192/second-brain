# BDH

Dragon Hatchling. Graph + Hebbian LLM. Authors say it matches GPT-2 scaling 10M–1B and gives local "equations of reasoning."

Source: [[src-arxiv-2509-26507]]. Project: [[arxiv-tierlist]].

## Kept

BDH-GPU: low-rank factors, linear attention, positive ReLU, about \((3+o(1))nd\) parameters. Working memory is synaptic state. Brain link is expressiveness, not a wet experiment.

Long-horizon PAC / thermodynamic-limit bounds are aspirational. Scaling curves were not re-plotted here. §§5–7 and appendix proofs unread.
