# Views vs sales

Views ≠ sales.

Source: [[src-deronin-views-vs-sales-2026-08-27]]. Chess pair also in `growth/growth-insights.md`: 74k views / 51 bought vs 135k views / 2 bought.

Content that sells is not content that only pulls views.
