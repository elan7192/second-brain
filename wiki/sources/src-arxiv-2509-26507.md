- Authors: Kosowski, Uznański, Chorowski, Stamirowska, Bartoszkiewicz (Pathway). cs.NE. 62 pages. 2025-09-30.

## Claims kept

BDH: n neuron particles, Hebbian fast weights, local graph dynamics. BDH-GPU is the trainable case. Authors claim GPT-2-class scaling 10M–1B on the same data. "Equations of reasoning" and brain link are expressiveness arguments, not a neuroscience experiment.

PAC-style long-horizon thermodynamic-limit bounds are a goal, not a theorem proved here. Later experiment figures, §§5–7, and appendix proofs/code were not fully read. Do not treat those as known.
