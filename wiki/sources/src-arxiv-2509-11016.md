## Claims kept

SuperDE: DDQN picks one of 4 DE mutations and 7 CHTs each generation (28 actions) from a 22-d handcrafted state. Trained on 18 CEC2010 COPs. Often wins Wilcoxon on CEC2010-ext / CEC2017 / G2000 / BBOB2022 vs listed DE variants. Still a small Q-net over a fixed operator pool, not a general foundation model.

Several CEC2010-ext problems stay fully infeasible for all methods. Limitations they state: no discrete/combinatorial COPs; discrete hand-designed state and action pool.
