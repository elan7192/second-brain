## Claims kept

Tony moved Codex automations to Grok Bot and points at the design team's philosophy (translates x.ai Designing Grok Bot).

From the design post (and CN article blocks): Grok Bot is designed for **persistent** agents beyond one chat — roster, presence, own computer, work that continues without a prompt.

Five primitives kept in the product surface: **Bots**, **Chats**, **Prompts** (once / Skills / Routines), **Tools**, **Artifacts**. Hide the rest until needed.

Sidebar organizes around a Bot roster, not disposable chat history. Presence answers who / what doing / how much to show.
