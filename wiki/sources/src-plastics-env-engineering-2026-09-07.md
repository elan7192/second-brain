## Claims kept

**Leakage atom** = measurable mass-flow of polymer/additive leaving an intended engineered pathway (year, polymer/size class, pathway, MFA method). Units t/yr (or items/yr for DRS).

**Not atoms:** circular-economy slogans; OECD/Pew scenario forecasts; corporate pledges; recycled-content **mass-balance** claims; LCA CO2e alone; “recycling rate” without denominator + measurement point (Conversio teaching: collected 17% / recycled 12% / PCR 9%).

**Accounting families are incommensurable** (do not collapse): OECD GPO leakage vs Conversio waste-fate vs Pew pollution scenarios vs SciAdv land–ocean–atmosphere — keep **separate columns**.

**Engineering modules (public capability map):** mechanical recycle · NIR/MWIR/AI sort · DRS · chemical recycle (PET depoly vs pyrolysis) · DfR · EPR · LCA — each with physics limits and failure modes.

**AI≠polymers hard wall** (align [[src-lan-e-ai-divide-atoms-2026-09-06]] · [[src-atom-manipulation-knowledge-map-2026-09-07]]): software can MRV claims, DfR schemas, DRS loops, sort assist, river-tail GIS; cannot substitute thermodynamics of C–C vs condensation polymers, collection trucks/dumps, tire abrasion, textile shed, PVC Cl, cheap virgin resin, persistence.

**Failure modes / greenwash:** rate illusion (collected ≠ recycled ≠ PCR); mass-balance free allocation as molecule fiction; pyrolysis scored as closed-loop without polymer-to-polymer yield; pre-consumer scrap chem-recycle sold as leakage abatement.

**STEAL (literacy / software-native):** claim integrity MRV; DfR schema (APR/RecyClass); measurement-point discipline; DRS data products; NIR/AI sort with black-plastic caveat; leakage-atom inventory; LCA allocation stress-test; Meijer river-tail prioritisation.

**SKIP:** staff env seat; “optimise” pyrolysis as circularity without mass balance; open-ocean cleanup as primary control; unify one global leakage number as atom.
