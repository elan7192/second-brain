## Claims kept

**Leakage atom** = measurable mass-flow of polymer/additive leaving an intended engineered pathway (year, polymer/size class, pathway, MFA method). Units t/yr (or items/yr for DRS).

**Not atoms:** circular-economy slogans; OECD/Pew scenario forecasts; corporate pledges; recycled-content **mass-balance** claims; LCA CO2e alone; “recycling rate” without denominator + measurement point.

**Three-column MFA (keep separate — do not collapse):** OECD GPO leakage · Conversio waste-fate · Pew pollution scenarios (+ SciAdv land–ocean–atmosphere as fourth when cited). Chem-recycle share of global production: Plastics Europe Fast Facts **0.1%** (keep).

**Engineering modules:** mechanical recycle · NIR/MWIR/AI sort · DRS · chemical recycle (PET depoly vs pyrolysis) · DfR · EPR · LCA — each with physics limits and failure modes.

### Fable METHOD amend (STEAL — curriculum / equation layer)

1. **Stock eqn + six levers:** `dM/dt = L − R − kM` (k≈0 for commodity PE/PP). Levers: reduce P; raise collection/closure; cycle; raise k only on leak-inevitable uses; raise R at high-concentration nodes; lower hazard h. AI claims must map to ΔP / Δη_c / Δη_x / Δy / Δk / ΔR + tonnes/MJ/years — else information-flow only.
2. **Four root causes:** C–C vs hydrolase; unpriced externality; sorting as entropy reduction; removal cost vs dilution.
3. **Hazard = M × h(size, shape, polymer, additives, sorption, pathway)** — fragmentation can raise harm at constant mass.
4. **Four recycling-rate defs that must multiply:** collect × sort × process × substitution. Teaching numbers Conversio **17 / 12 / 9** (collected / recycled / PCR in products).
5. **Circular-claim 5-point audit:** closed mass balance · functional unit · allocation rule · data layer · public third-party report.
6. **Instruments / facts to STEAL:** UK **Plastic Packaging Tax** (HMRC; £200/t from Apr 2022, indexed later; threshold <30% recycled) — separate from EPR; France **AGEC** washer-filter statute (Art. 79 / Climat Art. 47; modalities via decree — do not vault national capture %); **Wiesinger** ES&T 2021 >10k additives / >2.4k of concern (**separate** from UNEP Chemicals in Plastics 2023 counts — do not merge vintages); WWTP tertiary MP median **~95%** with **sludge transfer** (not “removed”); **Sentinel-2** 10 m = aggregations only (Biermann SciRep 2020).

**AI≠polymers hard wall** (align [[src-lan-e-ai-divide-atoms-2026-09-06]] · [[src-atom-manipulation-knowledge-map-2026-09-07]]): MRV / DfR / DRS / sort assist / river-tail GIS yes; cannot hydrolyse PE, substitute trucks/dumps, tire abrasion, textile shed, PVC Cl, cheap virgin, persistence.

**Failure modes / greenwash:** rate illusion; mass-balance free allocation; pyrolysis as closed-loop without P2P yield; pre-consumer scrap chem-recycle as fake leakage abatement; bio-based ⊥ biodegradable; WWTP “removal” = sludge transfer.

### DO NOT VAULT as facts (Fable mush / category errors)

- η_c≈78% / η_x≈90% as OECD collection/closure (invented split; OECD publishes mismanaged waste share / leakage shares, not those η).
- Kole ~6 Mt TWP **generation** as OECD **leakage** (OECD tyre abrasion leakage ~0.7 Mt of 2.7 Mt micro — different quantity).
- Textile 0.2–0.5 Mt as OECD wash **leakage** (OECD wash leakage 0.01 Mt; 0.2–0.5 is IUCN/EEA-class framing / generation conflict).
- Ocean cleanup **$100–1000/kg** as blended TOC point (ladder ranking OK; retune mid-ocean ~10–100 USD/kg class).
- L driven by **P** not **W** without the stock caveat (OECD use ~460 Mt vs waste W~353 Mt — ~100 Mt/yr to in-use stock is not leakage).
