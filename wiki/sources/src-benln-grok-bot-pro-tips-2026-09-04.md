## Claims kept

Ben Lang Sep 4 pack: seven Grok Bot tips with linked posts (capture truncated after #7).

Token path: GUI/browser once → capture network → call API next time. Prefer MCP/API over browserUse over computerUse. See [[src-samsokolin-browser-to-api]].

iOS trackpad mode is a product tip for phone/desktop control (@lingxi).

Host-machine access from phone lets a bot reach the user's computer remotely (@ericzakariasson).

Notification sounds are user-customizable under Settings > General > Notification Sounds (@mattyp).

Friction skim of recent chats (Eggbot / poteto framing) is a **pattern**: review recent chats for friction. Desk does **not** hire Eggbot; CoS owns that skim.

Sidebar organization (pin, resize, titles, sections) keeps multi-bot desks legible (@pengzheng_).

Webhook triggers wake bots from outside chat — event over poll. See [[src-ericzakariasson-webhook-wake]].
