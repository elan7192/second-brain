## Claims kept

**Pipeline:** `photo → candidates(identity, condition_grade, defect_boxes, confidence) → human/tech confirm → join comps by identity+condition+venue+time`.

Image **can** help coarse identity + visible cosmetic condition. Image **cannot** supply cleared sale, battery %, silent defects, true config/SKU when not visible, authenticity without provenance.

Price regression from pixels alone = **forecast aid**, not atom (same lesson as [[src-timesfm-principles-2026-09-06]] / Zestimate in [[src-valuation-anything-learn-from-others-2026-09-06]]).

**STEAL:** structured identity+condition hypotheses; multi-view capture; defect taxonomy before grade; OCR/diagnostics first-class; VLM for gross mismatch only; join clears; confidence gates + human escalate; privacy mask serials/faces.

**SKIP:** photo→TWD price model as SoR; buying CarDD/CDDM as product this week; training huge SKU net; treating SHPhoneBench DP prices as real clears (anonymous / unverified until peer review).
