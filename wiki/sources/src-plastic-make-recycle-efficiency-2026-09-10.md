## Claims kept

**Three tracks:** (A) virgin make monomer→polymer→converting + PCR re-entry points; (B) plant classes B1–B7 (MRF/NIR · wash/pellet · B2B food-grade · PET depoly · dissolution · pyrolysis · WTE); (C) efficiency axes before ranking.

**Efficiency axes:** mass yield (usable polymer or stated monomer-equivalent / waste in) · energy MJ/kg recyclate · food-grade path · $/kg · GHG · contamination tolerance · maturity. Define before compare.

**Uekert 2023 closed-loop (primary):** mechanical + glycolysis best economics/environment on clean streams; dissolution/enzymatic/methanolysis higher quality cost; pyrolysis→plastic monomer retention **0.01–0.14** class — DOI https://doi.org/10.1021/acssuschemeng.2c05497. Numeric bands = US model **估**, not TW quotes.

**TW path (實 narrative):** TFDA physical rPET case path; bottletobottle.org.tw list; Far Eastern ChemCycle pilot (公開單線產率/CapEx **缺**). Container recyclate-use ~5% = **policy magnitude**, not full mass closed-loop.

**EU:** (EU) 2022/1616 mechanical PCR-PET suitable tech #1; non-food input ≤5% — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32022R1616

**Hype gates:** chem ≠ always greener on mechanizable streams; pyrolysis oil ≠ circular plastic (plastic-to-oil vs plastic-to-plastic); enzyme not 2026 mass replace; recyclate-% ≠ closed loop; MRF sort ≠ end recycling rate; mass-balance free allocation; **functional-unit trap** (JRC waste-treated vs Uekert kg recyclate can reverse ranks). **Forbidden:** compare pyrolysis oil MJ to mechanical polymer MJ without cracker+poly+allocation.

**Decision intuition:** DfR + collection purity first (esp. TW 5% narrative); clean mono-PET/HDPE → mechanical / B2B first, do not chem-greenwash; PET-rich quality-reset → glycolysis preferred among chem; mixed dirty PO → dissolution / pyro-as-feedstock / WTE case-by-case; do not send clean bottle flake to pyrolysis.
